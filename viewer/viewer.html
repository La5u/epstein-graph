Privacy & FAQ

Does appearing here mean someone did something wrong?

No. Being named in these records implies no wrongdoing, no knowledge of any crime and no relationship with anyone. A link means only that names occur together in records. Nothing here is an accusation or a legal finding.

Where does this come from?

Documents released publicly by the US Department of Justice and related public records. Names, links and text are extracted automatically, so they contain errors: misread text, wrongly merged names and people who merely share a name. Check the source document before relying on anything.

How are survivors protected?

Survivors appear only under a pseudonym. Their names are not shown on this site, including those who have spoken publicly. No attempt is made to identify anyone the records keep anonymous.

Can I have a name removed or corrected?

Yes. Email lasushin@proton.me with the name or node and what is wrong, and say if it is about you. Requests are answered as soon as possible and within one month.

Your rights

If this site processes your personal data you have rights under the GDPR and the French Loi Informatique et Libertés: access, correction, erasure and objection. You can also complain to the CNIL (cnil.fr). Requests go to the address above.

Does the site track visitors?

The viewer itself sets no cookies and runs no analytics.
